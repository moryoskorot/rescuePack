# Asset choices for the expanded city

Checked 28 October 2026. The playable farm buildings and collection props are
built from Three.js geometry so they share the game's existing colors, lighting,
and scale. The following free packs are good candidates for more detailed
replacement art later.

| Pack | License | Best use | Fit notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Quaternius Farm Buildings Pack](https://quaternius.com/packs/farmbuildings.html) | CC0 | Barn, farmhouse, stable, well, fences | 13 farm models. Its older FBX/OBJ/Blend files need conversion to GLB before use. |
| [Kenney Nature Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit) | CC0 | Trees, crops, rocks, farm landscaping | 330 3D pieces. Check proportions against the existing Kenney City Kit before importing. |
| [Kenney Food Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/food-kit) | CC0 | Carrots and other food pickups | 200 3D pieces. Good first pack to inspect for the farm collection mission. |
| [Quaternius Fantasy Props MegaKit](https://quaternius.com/packs/fantasypropsmegakit.html) | CC0 | Books, tools, vegetables, supply boxes | Free tier includes glTF and shared texture atlases; style is more medieval than the city. |
| [Kenney Building Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/building-kit) | CC0 | More custom civic facades | Modular pieces can distinguish the clinic and schools, but need assembling. |
| [Kenney Generic Items](https://kenney.nl/assets/generic-items) | CC0 | 2D mission and inventory icons | 160 icons; useful for the phone UI rather than ground pickups. |

The playable truck now blends two recorded loops from [OpenGameArt's racing car
engine loops](https://opengameart.org/content/racing-car-engine-sound-loops)
(CC0). The original skid recording remains separate. See
`public/assets/LICENSE-audio.txt` for the included audio files and credits.
